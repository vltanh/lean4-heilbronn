## Positive coordinate products

The coordinate-product terminals use the proved rule of
[N6_BTTT_REMAINING_COORDINATE_PRODUCTS.md](N6_BTTT_REMAINING_COORDINATE_PRODUCTS.md).

\[
 P(x)=\sum_k n_k\prod_{i\in I_k}F_i(x)

reconstructed from its fixed earlier premises. Products use one or
two sources. The physical sources used at the terminal also appear
in the ordinary `Physical factors` table, before any coordinate

With the completed box \(\ell_i\le x_i\le u_i\), put
\(d_i=x_i-\ell_i\), \(w_i=u_i-\ell_i\), and expand exactly
\(P(\ell+d)=c_\varnothing+\sum_{m\ne\varnothing}c_md^m\).
Monomials are index multisets, including repeated indices. Then

\[
 P(x)\le c_\varnothing+
       \sum_{m\ne\varnothing,\ c_m>0}c_m\prod_{i\in m}w_i=:U.
\]

A negative nonconstant coefficient contributes at most zero; a
positive coefficient contributes at most its full width product.
The constant contributes its signed value. Each terminal prints
all expanded coefficients, all translated coefficients, every width
product and contribution, and a strictly negative exact \(U\).
That contradicts \(P(x)\ge0\). These products are used only after
their completed coordinate boxes and scalar sources have been proved.
