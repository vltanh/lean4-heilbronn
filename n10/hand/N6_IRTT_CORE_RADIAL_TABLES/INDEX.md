# IRTT core: three radial interval exclusions

These complete literal data prove the AB, BC and CQ radial exclusions,
without scratch files or a discovery module supplying their numbers.
Their geometry and mathematical source rules
are [core caps](../N6_IRTT_CORE_CAPS.md), [four radial cuts](../N6_IRTT_CORE_GAPS.md),
[initial domain](../N6_IRTT_CORE_DOMAIN.md), [octagon sources](../N6_IRTT_OCCUPIED_EAR.md), and the standard actual-area,
weighted-barycentric, support and multiaffine interval lemmas already cited
there. The coordinate order has twelve actual variables; no P is present.

Each file prints every retained physical factor, every coordinate endpoint
line, the full scalar stages and their immutable references, and every scalar
vertex-cap difference. A factor is retained exactly when a coordinate step
or final interval test uses it. The scalar stages are not dependency-pruned.

The [complete author reader](../N6_IRTT_CORE_RADIAL_check.py) checks every
premise and chain, as does the separate
[independent reader](../n6_irtt_core_independent.py). The
[HA proof](../N6_IRTT_CORE_HA.md) supplies
the fourth radial case; [the assembly](../N6_IRTT_CORE_COMPLETE.md)
excludes the whole IRTT count.

* [AB](AB.md): 2 stages, 157 coordinate steps, 1742 scalar updates, final target a.
* [BC](BC.md): 1 stages, 26 coordinate steps, 0 scalar updates, final target c.
* [CQ](CQ.md): 2 stages, 193 coordinate steps, 374 scalar updates, final target e.
