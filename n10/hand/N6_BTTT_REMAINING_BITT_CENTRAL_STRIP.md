# Positive-product form of the lower-central budget

This lemma supplies terminal products for the BITT proof. It excludes no
case by itself and introduces no new contact-order alternative.

Use the actual lower point \(P=(p,q)\) and contacts

\[
 A=(0,a),\quad B=(b,0),\quad C=(c,0),\quad H=(0,h),
 \qquad 0<a<h\le1,\quad0<b<c\le1.
\]

Assume the three actual central clearances

\[
 U=[HBP]-s=hp+bq-bh-s\ge0,\qquad
 V=[ACP]-s=cq+ap-ac-s\ge0,
\]
\[
 W=-[HCP]-s=hc-hp-cq-s\ge0.
\]

Put \(L=(h-a)(c-b)>0\) and \(R=3hc-ac-bh-ab\).
The exact polynomial identity is

\[
 \boxed{hcL-sR=c(h-a)U+h(c-b)V+(hc-ab)W.}                 \tag{1}
\]

Indeed the \(p\) coefficient of the right side is

\[
 ch(h-a)+ha(c-b)-h(hc-ab)=0,
\]

and its \(q\) coefficient is

\[
 bc(h-a)+hc(c-b)-c(hc-ab)=0.
\]

Its coefficient of \(-s\) is

\[
 c(h-a)+h(c-b)+hc-ab=R,
\]

and its remaining constant part is \(hc(h-a)(c-b)\).
Every multiplier on the right of (1) is positive: in particular

\[
 hc-ab=c(h-a)+a(c-b)>0.
\]

Thus (1) proves the common central budget of
[ONE_LOWER_POINT.md](ONE_LOWER_POINT.md), Section 3, directly as a
positive sum of three actual-area gaps. The two published cubic
relaxations have the exact positive-product forms

\[
 cL-sR=(hcL-sR)+c(1-h)(h-a)(c-b),                       \tag{2}
\]
\[
 hL-sR=(hcL-sR)+h(1-c)(h-a)(c-b).                       \tag{3}
\]

The additional terms are nonnegative, including \(c=1\) or \(h=1\).
