# One lower point, two central points and both side caps

Put \(s=93/1000\). Use the ten actual points and the closed square
conventions in [the domain lemma](C1_ONE_LOWER_TWO_CENTRAL_SIDES_GEOMETRY.md).
Thus Y is strictly inside OHC, Z,P are strictly inside HCDF,
Q is beyond CD in the right corner cap, and R is beyond HF in
the left corner cap. Every actual unsigned doubled triangle area
is at least s. The actual contact orders are d<=h and f<=c.

**Theorem.** These hypotheses are inconsistent.

This excludes the whole C1 count `(1,2,1,0,1)`, including its
transpose. The count is invariant under transposition, so h>=c
may be imposed. Independently exchange Z,P to arrange OZP>0.
Both choices are justified in the domain lemma, Section 1.
No other coordinate or determinant order is imposed.

The proof consists of that lemma, this note, and
[the complete literal index](C1_ONE_LOWER_TWO_CENTRAL_SIDES_TABLES/INDEX.md)
with every linked table. Its entries are fixed finite derivations;
no search result or external certificate is a premise.

## 4. Exact polynomial comparisons

Each physical source is the actual determinant expansion of a
proved geometric or scalar inequality. Its full rational polynomial
is printed before use. Source constants depending on a coordinate
box or scalar endpoint are fixed at their cited earlier stage.
The other primitive factors are the lower and upper gaps of the
completed coordinate box.

Write `x_i=l_i+(u_i-l_i)*z_i`, so `0<=z_i<=1`. Every printed
positive integer combination of products of nonnegative primitives
is nonnegative at the actual point. Repeated factors are permitted.
After exact expansion, a polynomial
`P(z)=C+sum c_m*z^m` has upper bound
`C+sum max(c_m,0)`, summing over its nonconstant monomials.
This holds at every degree, including repeated coordinate indices.
Every coefficient and the exact ceiling are printed. A negative
ceiling contradicts P>=0.

A global coordinate certificate also includes `W*z_j` for an upper
bound, or `-W*z_j` for a lower bound, with positive integer W.
The remaining printed sum is nonnegative. A ceiling U therefore
gives `z_j<=U/W` or `z_j>=-U/W`, respectively. Substitution gives
the printed actual-coordinate endpoint, rounded only outwards
when used by a later stage. These terminal polynomial products
are never interval-refinement premises.
