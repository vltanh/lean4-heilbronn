# Seven boundary points: all eight long triangles have capacity one

In the single-exterior N7 placement, every actual triangle in

    AEF, BEF, ABE, ABF, CDG, CGH, CDH, DGH

has doubled area strictly below 69/100. Consequently none contains both
remaining interior points: the two-interior-point triangle theorem requires
area at least Gamma2*s, where Gamma2=4+2sqrt(3) and s=93/1000, and
(149/20)s>69/100 with Gamma2>149/20.

This is a metric conclusion about the actual eight hull vertices, with no
assumption on the line joining the remaining two points. Eight fixed
polynomial sums with 3,212 positive integer terms complete the proof.
All their factors and rational boxes are specified below. The optional
standard-library checker `check_n7_long_triangle_caps.py` reads the printed
tables and reconstructs their arithmetic; no search or external data is a
premise.

## 1. Coordinates, previously proved bounds, and the height alternative

Use the single-exterior normalization of N7_OP_LINE_CAPS.md:

    A=(0,a), B=(b,0), C=(c,0), D=(1,d),
    E=(1,e), F=(f,1), G=(g,z), H=(0,h),

where A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H are in strict convex cyclic order,

    0<a<h<1, 0<b<c<1, 0<d<e<1,
    b<=f<=c, d<=h, a<=e, 0<g<f, h<z<1.

G is strictly above HF. Every cyclic actual hull determinant is at least
s. The other two actual points are inside the contact heptagon ABCDEFH.
All determinants and polygon areas in this note are doubled areas.

The six-ear argument in N7_PROJECTED_SIGNS.md already gives AEF,BEF<69/100
without a height restriction. If h>=e, project G vertically to G'=(g,1).
Then

    FG'H=(f-g)(1-h)>=FGH>=s,
    EFG'=(f-g)(1-e)>=(f-g)(1-h)>=s.

All other six boundary ears are unchanged. Applying the square symmetries
of N8_TRIANGLE_CAPS.md to this boundary octagon gives the eight listed
triangle bounds. Five triangles are unchanged; CDG,CGH,DGH increase under
projection, with derivatives 1-c,c,1 respectively. Thus all eight actual
bounds hold when h>=e. This argument uses only the eight projected ears,
not the locations of the remaining two points.

We may therefore assume h<=e for every fixed sum below. The targets are
CDG,CDH,ABE,ABF,DGH,CGH, each under its contradictory hypothesis that its
area is at least 69/100. DGH and CGH are each split at g=1/2.

## 2. A useful global bound: g<3/5

Put T0=f-g and W=EFH=(1-f)(1-h)+f(1-e). The exact identity

    (1-f)FGH+f EFG=T0 W

and the two actual ear bounds imply T0 W>=s. The paired-ear inequalities
of N7_OP_LINE_CAPS.md give h>=4s. Also CDE=(1-c)(e-d)>=s,
c>=f, and d>=93/790>117/1000 imply

    e>=117/1000+s/(1-f).

Suppose g>=3/5. Then f>3/5, T0<=f-3/5, and

    W<=1-4s+f(4s-117/1000)-s f/(1-f).

Here W>0 and 1-f>0. Multiplying the necessary inequality T0 W>=s by
1-f therefore gives B(f)>=0, where

    B(f)=(f-3/5)[(1-f)(1-4s+f(4s-117/1000))-s f]-s(1-f)
        =-(1275f^3+1565f^2-5003f+2349)/5000.

Set q=f-4/5>=-1/5. Direct expansion gives

    B=-51q^3/200-37q^2/40+51q/5000-1/5000
     <=-437q^2/500+51q/5000-1/5000
     <=-14879/87400000<0.

The first inequality uses 37/40+51q/200>=437/500; the second is completion
of the square. This contradiction proves g<3/5, independently of h<=e.

## 3. The closed rational boxes

Put

    eta=93/790, ell=7347/69700, Fmax=1-ell,
    h0=4s/Fmax, h1=1-s, f0=4s/(1-eta),
    x0=s/(h1-eta), g1=Fmax-s/(1-h0), t0=69/100.

Start with the following box, in coordinate order (a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,z).

| Coordinate | lower | upper |
|---|---|---|
| a | eta | 1-4s |
| b | x0 | Fmax |
| c | ell | Fmax |
| d | eta | h1 |
| e | h0 | 1 |
| f | f0 | Fmax |
| g | x0 | g1 |
| h | h0 | h1 |
| z | h0+s | 1 |

These are the proved rational ranges of N7_OP_LINE_CAPS.md Section 5.
The CDG and CDH rows use this box directly. For all other rows use the
following refinements; they are finite rational definitions of the printed
boxes, not a subdivision search.

Write l_j,u_j for the current lower and upper bounds on coordinate j.
Every replacement below retains the tighter of the old bound and the new
one: lower bounds are replaced by their maximum and upper bounds by their
minimum. The listed order is part of the definition.

First make the four common updates

    l_c <- f0,
    u_a <- 1-4s/Fmax,
    u_h <- 1-s/Fmax,
    u_d <- 1-s/(1-f0).

Then apply the appropriate target updates:

| Target | Additional replacements |
|---|---|
| ABE | l_b<-(t0-u_a)/(1-u_a), l_a<-7/25, l_e<-t0 |
| ABF | l_b<-(t0-u_a)/(1-u_a), l_a<-s/(Fmax+s-t0), l_f<-t0, l_c<-t0 |
| CGH | l_c<-t0, l_z<-t0/Fmax, l_g<-Fmax-(Fmax-t0)/l_h |
| DGH | u_d<-1-t0, l_z<-t0+eta, l_g<-(t0-1+l_h)/(l_h-eta) |

For a row ending `lo`, next impose u_g<=1/2. For a row ending `hi`, impose
l_g>=1/2 and u_g<=3/5, using Section 2. Finally perform the following
ordered list exactly twice, always using the latest values:

    1. l_b,l_g <- s/(u_h-l_a).
    2. l_h <- l_a+s/u_g;       u_h <- 1-s/(Fmax-l_g).
    3. u_g <- Fmax-s/(1-l_h);  l_f <- l_g+s/(1-l_h).
    4. l_c <- l_f;            l_e <- max(l_h,eta+s/(1-l_c),4s/(1-l_b)).
    5. u_d <- min(u_h,1-s/(1-l_f));  l_z <- l_h+s/Fmax.

All denominators in these particular eight boxes are positive, and every
resulting interval has 0<l_j<u_j<=1. These facts are also exact rational
checks. Here is why all updates hold geometrically. The common updates
use c>=f, f(1-a)>=4s, FGH<=Fmax(1-h), and DEF<=(1-f0)(1-d).
For the ABE and ABF targets, each area is at most a+(1-a)b. Also
ABC=a(c-b)>=s gives b<=Fmax-s/a, whence

    ABF<=Fmax+s-s/a,
    ABE<=Fmax+s+(1-Fmax)a-s/a.

The latter expression is increasing in a and is less than t0 at a=7/25.
Moreover ABE<=e and ABF<=f<=c. For the other two targets,

    CGH=c(z-h)+gh<=cz<=min(c,Fmax*z),
    CGH<=Fmax-(Fmax-g)l_h,
    DGH=z-h+g(h-d)<=z-d<=1-d,
    DGH<=1-l_h+g(l_h-eta).

These prove all target updates. The five repeated lines follow, in order,
from ABH=b(h-a)>=s and AGH=g(h-a)>=s; then AGH>=s and
FGH<=(f-g)(1-h); the same FGH bound; c>=f, e>=h, CDE>=s and
e(1-b)>=4s; finally d<=h, DEF>=s, and FGH<=f(z-h)<=Fmax(z-h).
Thus no exact box relies on an assumed sign of an interior-point triangle.

## 4. Every factor in the fixed sums is nonnegative

For a row's box let

    L_(2j)=coordinate_j-l_j, L_(2j+1)=u_j-coordinate_j  (j=0,...,8).

The remaining affine factors, in exact index order, are:

| Index | Factor | Index | Factor |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 18 | h-a-s | 19 | 697/790-h+a |
| 20 | e-d-s | 21 | 697/790-e+d |
| 22 | c-b-117/1000 | 23 | 79/100-c+b |
| 24 | f-b | 25 | c-f |
| 26 | f-g | 27 | h-d |
| 28 | e-a | 29 | z-h-s |
| 30 | e-h | | |

All these follow from normalization and the preceding bounds. A symbol
`XYZ+` means XYZ-s, with letters in their A,...,H order. Every such factor
is nonnegative because these are actual cyclic hull triangles. `BAD` is
the row's target triangle minus 69/100.

The support factors, already proved in N7_OP_LINE_CAPS.md Sections 2 and 5,
are

    EP=f(1-a)-4s,       EL=hc-4s,       ER=e(1-b)-4s,
    SUP=f^2(z-a)-fg(1-a)-4s(f-g),
    P_AHGFE=f(1-e)+(1-f)(1-a-11s),
    P_BCDEF=e(1-f)+(1-e)(1-b-11s),
    P_DEFGH=g(h-d)+z-h-11sg,
    P_HABCD=(h-11s)(1-c)+dc,
    P_ABCDE=(e-11s)b+a(1-b).

The five P factors are cleared actual-endpoint support-triangle bounds
for five convex actual vertices; the scale-free endpoint theorem requires
those containers to have doubled area greater than 11s. Their stated
nonnegative versions follow without any extra height condition.

Write area(IJKL)=IJK+IKL. The metric factors

    UABCD=8s-area(ABCD), UCDEF=8s-area(CDEF),
    UEFGH=8s-area(EFGH), UGHAB=8s-area(GHAB),
    UBR=9s-e(1-b), UAEF=69/100-AEF, UBEF=69/100-BEF

are nonnegative by N7_OP_LINE_CAPS.md and the six-ear long-triangle bounds.
Only row CGHhi additionally uses

    UDGH=69/100-DGH.

This is justified by the two earlier DGH rows and the h>=e argument in
Section 1. Thus the table order has no circular dependency.

Finally, the three paired-ear inequalities are

    sqrt(ABC)+sqrt(ABH)<=sqrt(hc),
    sqrt(BCD)+sqrt(CDE)<=sqrt(e(1-b)),
    sqrt(AGH)+sqrt(FGH)<=sqrt(S'),
    S'=f(fz-g)/(f-g)-fa<=1.

Since all six ears are at least s, each is at most (1-sqrt(s))^2<121/250.
Indeed s-(609/2000)^2=1119/4000000>0 and
121/250-(1-609/2000)^2=1119/4000000>0. Therefore `EAR_XYZ` denotes the
nonnegative factor 121/250-XYZ for any of these six ears. Squaring the
paired inequalities and using sqrt(XY)>=s gives the three further factors

    PAIR_BL=hc-ABC-ABH-2s,
    PAIR_BR=e(1-b)-BCD-CDE-2s,
    PAIR_TL=f^2(z-a)-fg(1-a)-(f-g)(AGH+FGH+2s).

The last line is the corresponding S' inequality multiplied by f-g>0.
Every printed product is consequently nonnegative under its row's
contradictory metric assumption.
