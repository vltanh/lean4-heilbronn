# The negative AFP half of the normalized central BTTT top word is impossible

Use the ten actual points, contact order, square-side conventions,
and minimum unsigned doubled area \(s=93/1000\) of
[N6_TOP_TRIPLE_SETUP.md](N6_TOP_TRIPLE_SETUP.md), with \(e\le f\).
The actual point \(P\) is inside \(HABC\); the actual points
\(Q,R,S\) are in the closed top cap, strictly on its corner side
of \(EF\). Assume the central top word

\[
 EQR,EQS,ERS>0,\quad FQR>0,\quad FQS<0,\quad FRS<0,
 \quad QRS>0,
\]

and the lower-central sign \(HBP>0\), equivalently \(BHP<0\).

**Theorem.** The additional actual-sign alternative \(AFP<0\)
is impossible.

The proof consists of the argument below and the literal tables in
[the index](N6_BTTT_REMAINING_CENTRAL_NEGATIVE_TABLES/INDEX.md),
[the geometric contexts](N6_BTTT_REMAINING_CENTRAL_NEGATIVE_TABLES/contexts.md),
and the 112 stage files linked from that index. Each retained
actual-sign deduction, physical polynomial, endpoint line, scalar
identity, immutable source reference, positive affine factor, and
contradictory leaf is printed. Separate exact readers and independent
geometric review accept the complete stated theorem.

Put \(\alpha=1037/10000\), \(K=149/20\), \(J=4427/500\),
\(G=1457/250\), and \(\delta=s/(1-\alpha)\). The geometric
and occupancy rules are those of the setup and
[N6_BTTT_REMAINING_TRIANGULAR.md](N6_BTTT_REMAINING_TRIANGULAR.md).
In particular, actual triangle occupancies zero through three have
costs \(s,3s,Ks,Js\). Four interior points in an actual triangle
of doubled area at most one are impossible, and a three-point
occupancy has the published distributed mesh.

The central top hull \(EQRF\), with \(S\) inside, gives
\((1-e)(1-f)\ge Gs\). The actual \(ABC,ABH\) clearances give
\(a,b>\alpha\), hence \(e,f>\alpha\). Consequently

\[
 a,b,e,f\le88574/224075,\qquad a,e\le33/125.
\]

The second ceiling follows from \(e\le f\) and the exact gap
\(Gs-(1-33/125)^2=77/250000>0\). The actual triangles
\(ABC,ABE,ACE,BCE\) have areas at most \(a,e,e,e\), respectively,
so all four are empty because \(33/125<3s\). These signs force
\(ACP>0\). Thus both lower-central signs hold and \(hc\ge Gs\).

The coarse coordinate bounds, inward tests, fixed cross signs,
empty-triangle deductions, five-label sign identity, convex-hull
affinity, and distributed-three mesh are exactly those used in the
accepted positive-\(AFP\) theorem
[N6_BTTT_REMAINING_CENTRAL_POSITIVE.md](N6_BTTT_REMAINING_CENTRAL_POSITIVE.md).
Every context gives its own complete trace and final actual-sign
table. A listed branch sign enters only in its own case and its
descendants. The five capacity signs in the index enter only after
their named upper-area sources have been proved.

Every physical coordinate factor is one of: a signed actual area
minus its witnessed occupancy cost; a contact-order difference;
the lower endpoint cost \(hc-Gs\); the central top cost;
a proved earlier bound on the same actual unsigned area; a
containing-anchor affine cap minus the witnessed occupancy cost;
or

\[
 h(c-b)p-s(c+b)\ge0. \tag{1}
\]

Equation (1) follows from the exact identity
\(c[HBP]+b[CHP]=h(c-b)p\), the positive actual determinants
\([HBP],[CHP]\ge s\), and \(b,c>0\). The containing-anchor
caps are the expanded contact-triangle bounds of
[N6_TOP_TRIPLE_SETUP.md, occupied-anchor affine caps](N6_TOP_TRIPLE_SETUP.md#occupied-anchor-affine-caps).
An actual triangle whose vertices lie in that proved containing
anchor triangle has area at most its area. The printed physical
factor is therefore the selected affine cap minus the applicable
actual occupancy floor. Every coordinate update lists all distinct
endpoint lines and applies the exact multiaffine rule, with outward
rounding to multiples of \(10^{-6}\).

The scalar rules are actual fan sums, the signed four-point identity,
unsigned five-label identities, occupied-quadrilateral area floors,
endpoint-support inequalities, and signed-area barycentric intervals.
For four actual distinct labels the added linear identity is

\[
 [ABC]+[ACD]-[ABD]-[BCD]=0. \tag{2}
\]

After the four actual signs are known, its unsigned-area coefficient
is the displayed sign in (2) multiplied by the sign of the actual
determinant. Solving (2) for a target unsigned area and choosing
already proved endpoints according to each coefficient gives every
printed `four` update. Initial bounds are witnessed occupancy floors,
exact coordinate-box maxima capped by one, proved earlier bounds,
or an exact affine maximum for a proved containing anchor triangle.
Each ordinary scalar update prints both its exact result and its
outward rational rounding.

Further scalar bounds and eleven terminal contradictions use
[the exact affine bound lemma](N6_BTTT_REMAINING_AFFINE_BOUNDS.md).
That lemma proves nonnegativity of all six printed factor families:
area bounds, signed actual fan and four-point identities,
occupied-quadrilateral floors, product envelopes for actual
five-label identities, support envelopes, and affine signed-area
barycentric bounds. The one-occupant ordinary support rate may be
\(G\), since \(G<3+2\sqrt2\). Each application prints its actual
relation witness, every fixed endpoint source, the expanded affine
factor, and a positive integer weight.

For clarity, if the displayed positive sum is
\(L=C+\sum_T c_TA_T\ge0\), its ceiling apart from the target
\(V\) is

\[
 U_{\ne V}=C+\sum_{T\ne V,\,c_T>0}c_Tu_T
                  +\sum_{T\ne V,\,c_T<0}c_T\ell_T.
\]

Thus \(c_V>0\) gives \(A_V\ge-U_{\ne V}/c_V\), and
\(c_V<0\) gives the corresponding upper bound. The tables print
that exact quotient and its outward rounding. At a terminal the
full affine ceiling is strictly negative. The factors and integer
weights prove these conclusions directly; no optimization or
numerical feasibility assertion is a premise.

Every area bound has an immutable `stage:row` identifier. A scalar
update cites earlier rows in the same stage. An affine-bound output
comes after all ordinary updates in that stage, and every source
row precedes its output. Its product and barycentric envelopes use
the fixed endpoint versions in their printed witness, including
sources whose coefficients later cancel. A later physical factor
or initial bound may cite only a completed preceding stage of the
same actual-sign case or one of its ancestors. Every subsequent
coordinate box intersects its own coarse box with the completed
box named in `Parent box`. A sibling case never supplies a source.
These rules prevent circular endpoints or future sign assumptions.
