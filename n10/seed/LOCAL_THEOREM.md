# Imported exact construction and unrestricted local theorem

These are the mathematical inputs of the previous n=10 package. This file
records the proof needed by global_cover.cpp; no global completion is inferred.
The existing asymmetric chamber is not needed for the new unsigned-area cover.

Let g be the unique real root of f=12g^3-27g^2+20g-4, a=1-3g+2g^2,
and A=5g^2/8-g^3/2. The attaining configuration, labeled A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H,O,P,
is `(0,a),(a,0),(1-g/2,0),(1,g/2),(1,1-a),(1-a,1),(g/2,1),
(0,1-g/2),(g,g),(1-g,1-g)`. The construction is the prior Comellas-Yebra
configuration, reconstructed in Sudermann-Merx arXiv:2603.11107v3, Table 13.
`verify_n10.py` checks all 120 absolute areas in Q(g), with sixteen equalities.
The sixteen signed active areas in alphabetical order are
`ADO+, AEO-, AEP+, AGH+, AOP+, BCD+, BFO+, BFP-, BGO-, BOP-, CDE+,
CFP+, EHP+, EOP-, FGH+, FOP+`.

## Theorem

For any labeled configuration in the square at coordinate sup-distance
`0<h<=1/2000` from the candidate, its minimum triangle area is strictly below A.
All twenty coordinates may move; no boundary incidences or symmetry are fixed.

## Exact first-order data

Let J be the sixteen gradients and N the eight inward coordinate normals.
The verifier constructs positive weights lambda and mu such that
`sum(lambda_T J_T)+sum(mu_i N_i)=0`. Triangle weights are
`L=24g^2-30g+8` for ADO,BGO,CFP,EHP;
`M=12g^2-27g+8` for AGH,BCD,CDE,FGH; and 1 for the other eight.
Boundary weights are `6g^2-7g+2` at A,B,E,F and `26g^2-39g+10` at C,D,G,H.

The common kernel is generated by the motion v:
A=(0,2(1-g)), B=(2(1-g),0), C=(-1/2,0), D=(0,1/2),
E=(0,-2(1-g)), F=(-2(1-g),0), G=(1/2,0), H=(0,-1/2),
O=(1,1), P=(-1,-1). Direct substitution gives Jv=Nv=0 and ||v||inf<11/8.

The 38 exact duals in local_dual_data.py prove:
`Je>=-epsilon`, `Ne>=0`, `e_Ox=0`, `epsilon>=0` imply `||e||inf<=81 epsilon`.
For each coordinate and sign, nonnegative algebraic coefficients on rows of
(J,N) represent minus that signed coordinate, up to the gauge coordinate.
The coefficients on J sum to less than 81. All row identities, signs, and sums
are reconstructed exactly. Taking epsilon=0 also proves the kernel assertion.
The data file is compressed only for transport; running it prints ordinary JSON.

## Second-order proof

Write each signed active area as `A+J_T d+q_T(d)` exactly. If h=||d||inf,
then `|q_T(d)|<=2h^2`: the three displacement vectors lie in a square of side 2h.
Each selected signed area remains positive in the stated neighborhood because
its gradient row sum is <=3, A>1/25, and `3/2000+2/2000^2<1/25`.

Assume all actual areas >=A. Then Jd>=-2h^2. Put q=d_Ox and e=d-qv.
The dual lemma gives E=||e||inf<=162h^2<=81h/1000, and
`|q|<=h`, `|q|>=(h-E)/(11/8)>=919h/1375`.

Let Q(d)=sum(lambda_T q_T(d))=d^T H d. Exact reconstruction proves
`Q(v)=-54g^2+195g/2-36 < -21/2`,
`2 sum_i |(Hv)_i|<32`, and `sum_ij |H_ij|<28`. Hence

`Q(d)<=-(21/2)q^2+32|q|E+28E^2`
`<=-(57921081/30250000)h^2<0`.

But the positive stress and Nd>=0 give
`0<=sum lambda_T(F_T(P)-A)=-sum mu_i(Nd)_i+Q(d)<=Q(d)<0`.
This contradiction proves the theorem and its strict equality statement.
