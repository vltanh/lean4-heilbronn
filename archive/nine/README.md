# Nine points: archived draft

The first draft of the nine-point proof, written by ChatGPT 6 Pro on 14 and
15 November 2026, with its research notes and the scripts that exported its
certificates. It is kept for the record only. The Lean proof in
`Heilbronn/Nine/` stands on its own, nothing outside this folder refers to it,
and it is not maintained: its paths are those of the repository before it was
archived.

- `proof/`: the draft.
- `research/`: the research notes, audits and searches behind it.
- `scripts/`: the exporters and searches that turned its certificates into
  Lean modules and data files.
- `data/`: exported files that the Lean proof does not read.
